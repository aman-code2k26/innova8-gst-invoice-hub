Slide 3 · Features (1 of 2)

FR-1 → FR-3: Setup, smart billing & PDF

FR-1 Profile & clients

  • Save business name, PAN, GSTIN, address
  • Store bank UPI ID for payments
  • Client address book with GSTIN + state

Demo: Settings → save profile → Clients → add a client.

FR-2 Invoice builder

  • Line items: qty, hourly/fixed rate, discount
  • Auto CGST+SGST (same state) vs IGST (other state)
  • LUT toggle → 0% GST for exports
  • HSN/SAC codes, round-off, live totals

Demo: change client state, watch tax type flip automatically.

FR-3 PDF generator

  • One click → downloadable PDF with your logo
  • Indian tax-invoice layout: parties, HSN, split tax
  • Generates in < 1 second (requirement)

Demo: Save & download PDF → open the file on stage.

Non-functional: GST-compliant format < 1 sec PDF generation Bank-grade protection of banking details

⚙ GST logic (the smart part)

Client state
vs
Your state
→
Same? CGST+SGST
Different? IGST
Export? LUT 0%
  • Taxable = qty × rate − discount
  • GST = taxable × rate (5 / 12 / 18 / 28%)
  • Grand total rounded to nearest rupee, difference shown as round-off
  • Amount goes into the UPI QR automatically
